      Terms of Service

      Last updated: June 2026

      Welcome to UniVibe. By accessing or using our platform, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. Please read them carefully before using the service.

      1. About UniVibe

      UniVibe is a campus event booking platform that allows university students and the general public to discover, book, and attend events hosted by universities and student organizations across Kenya.

      2. Eligibility

      You must be at least 16 years old to use UniVibe. By booking a ticket, you confirm that the information you provide — including your name, phone number, and student registration number — is accurate and truthful.

      3. Ticket Bookings

      • Tickets are issued on a first-come, first-served basis.
      • A ticket is valid for the specific event, date, and tier it was booked for.
      • Tickets may not be transferred, resold, or duplicated.
      • UniVibe reserves the right to cancel a booking if fraudulent activity is detected.
      • Each ticket is identified by a unique ticket code and QR code — keep it safe.

      4. Cancellations & Refunds

      You may cancel your ticket directly from the "My Tickets" section at any time before the event date. Cancellations release your booked slot for other attendees. As this platform currently does not process payments, no monetary refunds apply. For paid events, any refund policy is governed by the event organizer directly.

      5. Waitlist

      If an event is fully booked, you may join the waitlist. Being on a waitlist does not guarantee a ticket. You will be contacted if a slot becomes available.

      6. Code of Conduct

      By attending events booked through UniVibe, you agree to behave in a respectful and lawful manner. Any harassment, disruption, or misconduct at events may result in removal and permanent suspension from the platform.

      7. Limitation of Liability

      UniVibe acts solely as a booking platform. We are not responsible for event cancellations, changes, or quality issues caused by the event organizer. We are not liable for any loss, injury, or damage incurred while attending an event.

      8. Changes to These Terms

      We may update these Terms of Service from time to time. Continued use of the platform after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the new terms.

      9. Contact

      If you have any questions about these Terms, please contact us at support@univibe.co.ke.

      Privacy Policy

      Last updated: June 2026

      Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how UniVibe collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our platform.

      1. Information We Collect

      When you book a ticket, we collect the following information:

      • Full Name — to identify your booking
      • Phone Number — as your booking identifier and for lookup
      • Registration Number — for school events only, to verify student status
      • School / Faculty — for school events, to confirm eligibility

      We do not collect your email address, national ID, or payment card details.

      2. How We Use Your Information

      • To generate and issue your unique event ticket
      • To enable you to look up your bookings using your phone number
      • To allow event organizers to check in attendees via QR code
      • To maintain waitlists and contact you if a slot opens up
      • To display anonymized booking statistics (e.g. total tickets sold)

      3. Data Storage

      All data is stored securely in Google Firestore, hosted on Google Cloud. We apply industry-standard security rules to limit access to your data. Only authorized administrators can view individual booking records.

      4. Data Sharing

      We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared with:

      • Event organizers — solely to verify attendance at their event
      • Google (Firebase/Firestore) — as our data storage provider

      5. Data Retention

      Booking records are retained for up to 12 months after the event date, after which they may be deleted or anonymized. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

      6. Cookies & Analytics

      UniVibe uses Firebase Analytics to collect anonymized usage statistics (e.g. page views, device type). No personally identifiable information is included in analytics data. We do not use advertising cookies.

      7. Your Rights

      You have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@univibe.co.ke. We will respond within 7 business days.

      8. Changes to This Policy

      We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We encourage you to review it occasionally. Continued use of UniVibe after changes means you accept the updated policy.

      9. Contact

      For any privacy concerns, email us at support@univibe.co.ke.
